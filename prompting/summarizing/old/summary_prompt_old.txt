You are extracting factual information from a court opinion. The court opinion is provided in `opinion.pdf`.

Your task is to extract factual information about the crime and its execution in the form of a crime script, as well as explicitly stated characteristics of the primary suspect.

Do not infer, speculate, estimate, or add information that is not explicitly supported by `opinion.pdf`.

### Suspect Information

Extract the following characteristics of the primary suspect of the case when they are explicitly stated in the opinion and place them in the `suspect-information` field:

- Age:
  - `0-18y`
  - `19-24y`
  - `25-29y`
  - `30-39y`
  - `40-49y`
  - `50+y`

- Sex:
  - `Male`
  - `Female`

- Economic Status:
  - `Lower`
  - `Middle`
  - `Upper`

  Use the following income thresholds:
  - Lower: income < $41,392
  - Middle: $41,392 ≤ income ≤ $124,176
  - Upper: income > $124,176

- Education Level:
  - `High school`
  - `Bachelor’s`
  - `Above Bachelor’s`

Use only the categories specified above. Do not infer any characteristic from indirect information.

For age, use an explicitly stated age or date of birth when available. If a date of birth is provided, use it only to determine the appropriate age category; do not include the date of birth anywhere in the `crime-script` field.

For economic status, classify the suspect only when sufficient explicit income information is provided to determine the appropriate category. Do not infer economic status from occupation, residence, clothing, possessions, or other indirect characteristics.

For educational level, classify the suspect only when the opinion explicitly provides sufficient information about their education. Do not infer educational level from occupation or other indirect characteristics.

If a characteristic cannot be determined from information explicitly stated in the opinion, return `null`.

### Information Separation

The `crime-script` field represents the factual information that will be provided to a separate AI for suspect-profile prediction.

Therefore, **DO NOT include the suspect's age, sex, economic status, or educational level anywhere in `crime-script`**, even if these characteristics are explicitly stated in the opinion.

These four characteristics must appear **only** in `suspect-information`.

Do not indirectly reveal these characteristics through information such as:

- Date of birth
- Exact age
- Gender or sex
- Income or salary
- Educational institution
- Degree information
- Graduation information
- Any other information that directly reveals or allows the four characteristics to be determined

The purpose of this separation is to ensure that `crime-script` contains only information available to the separate AI when it predicts the suspect profile.

### General Extraction Rules

1. Use only information explicitly stated in `opinion.txt`.
2. Do not infer, speculate, estimate, or assume missing information.
3. If a requested field cannot be determined from the opinion, use `null`.
4. Extract factual information about the crime and its execution, not the court's legal analysis or conclusions.
5. Preserve the factual meaning of the opinion while summarizing information concisely.
6. Do not include information about the suspect's personality, intelligence, character, or psychological characteristics unless explicitly requested by the template.
7. Do not include irrelevant procedural history or legal arguments unless they contain factual information directly relevant to the crime.
8. Distinguish between established facts and allegations. Do not present an allegation as an established fact unless the opinion establishes it as fact.
9. Information about victims, witnesses, accomplices, or other individuals may be included in `crime-script` when it is factually relevant to understanding the crime.
10. **Anonymize all individuals mentioned in the output. Do not include real names, initials, aliases, usernames, or other personally identifying names. Refer to individuals using generic labels such as `the defendant`, `the victim`, `the witness`, `the accomplice`, or `Person 1` when a generic label is insufficient to distinguish between individuals.**
11. Do not fill missing information using general knowledge or assumptions.
12. When multiple suspects are discussed, identify the primary defendant/suspect to whom the extracted `suspect-information` applies. Do not combine characteristics from different individuals.
13. Return **valid JSON only**. Do not include explanations, commentary, or Markdown outside the JSON object.

### Output Format

Your output should be a single JSON file named `case_summary.json`, exactly following the format of `summary_template.json`.
